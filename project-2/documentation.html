Noteworthy

Visualizer Design and Composition:

  • I created a dynamic scene for the audio visualizer by editing a donut shop illustration and splitting it into multiple pieces to be drawn one at a time
  • I gave multiple elements of the scene effects that react to the music being played
  • I added the light flicker of the lampost to make the scene more alive
  • I used multiple canvases to display the audio visualizer bars. Each canvas I then warped with a perspective transform to make them match up with the perspective of the donut shop's LED screens. Because of this, fullscreening the background canvas removes the bars and moving the background canvas causes the bars to no longer line up

User Controls:

  • I implemented multiple parameters for the user to control so they can customize the scene how they see fit
  • Users can greatly change the look and behavior of the 2 types of rain used in the visualizer
  • Users can turn on and off the additional effects to objects in the scene

Note Detection:

  • I used a simplified form of onset detection to create the rain and city light effects that synch up to prominent beats in the music
  • The city lights in the background of the scene change colors with significant notes, or frequency jumps, in the music while the rain is synched to a user-controllable threshold of frequency jumps

Additional Effects:

  • The donut light on top of the shop scales with the bass and lower frequencies of the song
  • The lampost light flickers by giving an image a random alpha value between 0 and 1 until that random value is greater than .99 or less than .01. This also happens at random intervals
